Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Schwab 3-Month Investment Program?

Schwab is running an investment program designed to help our users grow with us. 1. Buy a Schwab Stock by depositing to invest with any amount. 2. Invest and watch your capital grow in 3 months — tripled. 3. After 3 months, withdraw your funds or decide to continue investing with us. Start investing today!

How do I open an account?

Click "Open Account" and complete our registration process. You'll need your personal information including SSN, address, and date of birth. Account activation is quick and easy.

How much does it cost to work with Schwab?

$0 online listed stock, option, and ETF trade commissions. Thousands of no-transaction-fee mutual funds. No charge to work with a Schwab Financial Consultant.

Is my money safe at Charles Schwab?

Under the Schwab Security Guarantee, Schwab will cover losses in any of your Schwab accounts due to unauthorized activity. Your funds are secure with us.

What investment products does Schwab offer?

Schwab offers stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, options, bonds, CDs, fixed income, annuities, cryptocurrency, and more to help you build a diversified portfolio.

How do I deposit funds?

You can deposit funds via Bitcoin (BTC). Bank transfer and card deposit options are coming soon. Bitcoin deposits may take a while to reflect in your account.

How to Deposit

Bitcoin Deposit

1. Navigate to the Deposit section from your dashboard.

2. Select Bitcoin (BTC) as your deposit method.

3. Enter the USD amount you wish to deposit.

4. The system will display the equivalent in Bitcoin at the current rate.

5. Click Proceed to view the Bitcoin wallet address and QR code.

6. Send your Bitcoin to the provided address.

7. Click "I have Paid" to mark the transaction as pending.

8. Wait for confirmation. This may take a while before it reflects.
