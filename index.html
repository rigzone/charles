FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our shipping and tracking services.

How can I track my shipment?
Enter your tracking number in the tracking box at the top of this page and click "Track Now." You'll see real-time status, route map, and delivery progress for your shipment.
What areas does Greenway Express cover?
We operate across 180+ countries with coverage on six continents. Our network includes 450+ port connections, 120+ airport hubs, and 75+ distribution centers worldwide.
How long does international shipping take?
Transit times vary by service type and route. Air freight typically takes 1-3 days for international shipments, while ocean freight ranges from 7-30 days depending on the route.
Can I change my delivery address?
Address changes may be possible depending on the shipment status. Contact our support team as soon as possible, and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.
What happens if my package is delayed?
In the rare event of a delay, our tracking system will update your shipment status in real-time. Our team proactively monitors all shipments and will work to resolve any issues immediately.
Do you offer insurance for shipments?
Yes, all shipments are fully insured during transit. We offer comprehensive coverage options tailored to the value and nature of your cargo for complete peace of mind.
