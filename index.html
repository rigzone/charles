Enter your Container number, Bill of Lading, or Booking number into the Tracking widget at the top of this page. You can track up to 3 shipments at once by separating references with commas. Results update in real time showing status, location and estimated arrival.

Standard documentation includes a Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin and any applicable import/export licences. Requirements vary by origin/destination country. Our customer service team can guide you through country-specific requirements.

Standard customs clearance takes 1–5 business days depending on the destination country, cargo type and completeness of documentation. Pre-lodging documents before vessel arrival and using our End-to-End Solutions service can significantly reduce clearance times.

We ship virtually all cargo types including dry goods, refrigerated/perishable cargo, hazardous materials (with proper certification), oversized/project cargo, vehicles, breakbulk and liquid bulk. Contact our team for specialist cargo requirements.

Who We Are

A World Leader in Shipping & Logistics

Founded in Marseille in 1978 by Jacques Saadé, CMA CGM has grown into the world’s third-largest container shipping company. With a fleet of over 650 vessels and a presence in more than 170 countries through 400+ agencies, we connect businesses to the global economy.

From ocean freight and air cargo to logistics and end-to-end supply chain solutions, CMA CGM delivers smarter, more sustainable ways to move goods across the world. Our #BetterWays commitment drives us to invest in green fuels, digital innovation, and industry-leading service.
